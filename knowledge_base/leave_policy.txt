LEAVE POLICY

Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves per calendar year.

Employees are entitled to 10 sick leaves per calendar year.

Casual leave should normally be requested in advance and requires manager approval.

Sick leave maybe taken when an employee is unwell. The employee should inform the manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Unused casual leave cannot be carried forward to next calendar year.

Employees should submit leave requests through the organization's leave management system.
